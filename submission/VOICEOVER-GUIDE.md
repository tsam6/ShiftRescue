The video uses actual interface captures; it is not continuous cursor footage.

00s — ShiftRescue
Less time chasing coverage. More time serving the community. A volunteer coordination prototype for food pantries.

08s — A cancellation becomes someone’s to-do list.
Find a suitable person. Send a request. Follow up. Update the schedule. Informed by conversations with food assistance coordinators.

18s — Start with eligible backups.
The coordinator can see availability, qualification review, experience and opt-in. A nearby volunteer is not automatically an eligible volunteer.

28s — Keep review requirements intact.
Harper is excluded until qualification review is complete. Other exclusions show clear reasons. Eligibility is enforced by code, not guessed by a model.

37s — Approve the outreach pool.
Approval starts a simulated invitation to Blair and schedules a follow-up. The activity trail records the coordinator’s approval.

46s — Keep the next step visible.
Advance the replay clock by 10 minutes. One reminder is recorded for Blair. Time is accelerated for this demonstration.

55s — Volunteers make the decision.
The simulated inbox lets a volunteer explicitly accept or decline the invitation. An invitation alone never creates a booking.

63s — A decline moves outreach forward.
Blair declines. The next eligible, approved backup receives an invitation: Casey. The earlier reminder remains visible in the message log.

73s — Casey accepts. The gap is filled.
The workflow records the booking and stops pending follow-ups. The coordinator can see exactly who accepted.

83s — The Strands agent also ran successfully.
Real Amazon Bedrock calls advanced the workflow through tools and verified the saved booking. The browser replay is separate. Replies and delivery were simulated.

# Submission media

Use `ShiftRescue-polished-music.mp4` as the current video, or
`ShiftRescue-polished-silent.mp4` for adding your own narration. Both are 1:47,
1920×1080, 24 fps. The older draft videos are retained for reference.

The walkthrough uses actual local interface captures assembled into captioned
chapters. It is not continuous cursor footage. Its Bedrock evidence section refers
to the separately verified real-agent run. Simulated volunteers, messages, and
replay time are labeled in the footage and description.

The music is an original, programmatically synthesized instrumental track: sustained
chords and sparse plucked tones, with no vocals, sampled songs, or third-party music.
It is included under the repository's MIT license. The final mix is intentionally
quiet (approximately −27 dBFS mean) to leave room for a voiceover.
